# ZARP WhatsApp OTP 1.1.5

- Automatically links verified guest orders to a customer after successful WhatsApp OTP login or registration.
- Only claims orders that have no customer owner and contain this plugin's matching verified-phone metadata.
- Never moves orders that are already assigned to another customer.
- Adds an order note and a masked activity-log entry for every claim operation.
- Supports both new-account creation and existing-account passwordless login.
- Includes all fixes from versions 1.1.2 through 1.1.4.
