# ZARP WhatsApp OTP 1.1.2

- Makes WhatsApp OTP the exclusive customer login method when the feature is enabled.
- Hides the Wooma username, password, remember-me, login, lost-password, Google sign-in, and registration controls from the account side panel.
- Hides the standard WooCommerce login form on the My Account page while keeping the WhatsApp OTP interface available.
- Does not change checkout OTP verification or administrator login.
